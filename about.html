About the founder

Hands-on experience, not theory.

Keita Linton founded K North Technologies Inc. in Edmonton to help small businesses get real value from the Microsoft tools they already pay for.

Since 2020 Keita has worked in hands-on IT roles for Alberta businesses, including ATCO Electric and SideGroup: supporting Microsoft 365 users, managing Active Directory access, automating repeat work with PowerShell and investigating security alerts. Keita has also taught Microsoft technologies and cybersecurity as a Microsoft Instructor at Career City College, so explaining technology clearly is part of the job, not an extra.

  • 5+ years as an IT professional
  • Led company-wide Microsoft Copilot rollouts
  • Built PowerShell automation for organisation-wide deployments
  • Microsoft Instructor, Career City College
  • Diploma in IT, plus certificates in cybersecurity, security risk and network security
  • Teaches in plain English, with your real work, at your pace
