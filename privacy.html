Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Template for review. Have a lawyer or privacy adviser check this policy before the site goes live.

K North Technologies Inc. (“K North”, “we”, “us”) is based in Edmonton, Alberta. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, business name, email address, team size and message when you contact us or book a call.
  • Quiz and calculator answers: the readiness quiz and ROI calculator run entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us or stored by this website.
  • Client project information: when you hire us, we may access your Microsoft 365 settings and business systems only as needed to deliver the agreed work.

How the contact form works

This website does not store form submissions. The contact form opens your own email app with your message filled in; nothing is sent until you press send. Booking links may take you to a third-party scheduling service, which has its own privacy policy.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange calls.
  • To prepare proposals, deliver services and send invoices.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not use it for marketing without your consent, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in future, we will update this policy first.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business (for example email, scheduling and accounting tools), and only as needed. Some providers may store data outside Canada, where it may be subject to local laws.

Security and retention

We protect information with reasonable safeguards, including multi-factor sign-in and limited access. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, then delete it securely.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask questions about this policy. Contact our privacy contact at keita.linton@knorthtechnologies.ca. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
